LG washing machine suspension rod replacement

Washer vibration and balance-related suspension work.

By Alex Appliance Repair · Published September 9, 2026

Service focus

This LG washing-machine repair example documents suspension rod replacement. Four photographs show suspension work associated with vibration and balance concerns.

Work documented

The photo sequence records access to the suspension components and replacement work. The service concerns the system supporting the moving washer assembly.

Understanding this repair

Vibration can also be affected by the load, leveling, installation and other mechanical conditions. Report when movement occurs and whether it changes between loads. Persistent excessive movement needs evaluation; suspension parts should not be selected from a symptom alone.
