Problems we diagnose

Common microwave symptoms

A visible symptom identifies the place to begin. Model-specific testing determines whether the cause is a component, installation condition or utility connection.

Runs but does not heat

High-voltage components, controls and power delivery require trained testing because stored voltage can be dangerous.

Door will not latch

Hooks, switches, mounts and alignment must operate in the correct sequence for safe startup.

Turntable does not move

The motor, coupler, tray support and control output are checked after confirming the tray is seated.

Display or keypad is unresponsive

Power supply, membrane controls, user-interface boards and main controls can cause similar symptoms.

Cooling fan or cavity light fails

We evaluate cooling fans and cavity lighting in supported built-in microwave/wall-oven combination units.

Sparking inside the cavity

Metal contamination, damaged waveguide covers, racks or cavity damage must be identified before reuse.

Diagnosis before parts

A repair recommendation based on testing

We review the reported symptom, inspect the appliance and test the systems related to the failure. The result and estimate are explained before a repair is approved.

  • door switches and latches
  • turntable motors and couplers
  • control and display assemblies
  • cooling fans and cavity lights
  • fuses and power circuits
  • model-specific heating components

Safety note: Microwaves contain high-voltage components that can retain a dangerous charge after unplugging. Internal repair is not a do-it-yourself task.

Detailed service guide

Built-in Microwave and Wall Oven Combinations Only

Our built-in microwave + oven combo repair service covers a microwave integrated with a wall oven as one built-in combination appliance installed in cabinetry. These units have a microwave compartment and a separate oven compartment. A microwave mounted over a gas or electric range is not this type of appliance.

We do not repair over-the-range microwaves, countertop models, microwave drawers or standalone built-in microwaves. A trim kit or cabinet installation alone does not make a microwave eligible for this service. Share the model number and installation details so we can confirm support before scheduling.

Symptoms We Evaluate

Tell us if the microwave section will not start, runs without heating, stops during a cycle, shows an error, has an unresponsive display or has a door or turntable problem. Also mention whether the wall oven still works. The symptom guides diagnosis; it does not identify a failed part by itself.

On supported models, diagnosis may involve door interlocks, controls, power circuits, heating components, cooling fans and cavity lighting. The technician determines the appropriate checks for the exact model before recommending parts.

Prepare for a Built-in Appliance Service Visit

  • Provide the brand and complete model number if the label is safely accessible.
  • Describe which compartment is affected: microwave, oven or both.
  • Record error messages and when the problem occurs.
  • Mention cabinet trim, access restrictions and any previous work.

Do not remove the appliance from the cabinet or open its electrical covers. Internal microwave work requires professional handling. Stop using the appliance if it sparks, smokes, smells electrical or has a damaged door; do not repeat test cycles or bypass safety switches.

Repair or Replace the Combination Unit?

Compare the diagnosed repair and available parts with the complete installed cost of an appropriate replacement. Cabinet fit, the condition of both compartments and model support matter. A countertop microwave price is not a useful comparison for an integrated wall-oven combination, and a fixed age or price percentage cannot decide every case.

We confirm model support, explain the diagnosis and estimate, and check the appliance after approved work. We do not promise that every brand or model can be repaired.

Frequently asked questions

Built-in Microwave + Oven Combo Repair questions

Is a microwave worth repairing?

For a built-in microwave/wall-oven combination, repair options depend on diagnosis, appliance condition, parts availability and the installed cost of a suitable replacement. We explain the estimate before approved work.

Why does the microwave run without heating?

Several high-voltage or control faults can cause this symptom. Safe electrical testing is required.

Can a broken door switch stop the microwave?

Yes. Multiple interlock switches monitor the door, and a failed or misaligned switch can prevent operation.

Do you repair over-the-range or standalone microwaves?

No. Our built-in microwave/oven combo service is limited to built-in microwave/wall-oven combination units. We do not service over-the-range, countertop, drawer or standalone built-in microwaves.
