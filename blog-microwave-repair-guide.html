Built-in Microwave/Oven Combo: Repair or Replace?

Understand built-in microwave/oven combo symptoms, safe checks and repair decisions. Service excludes over-the-range and standalone microwaves.

Book service onlineMore repair guides

Confirm the Appliance Type Before Booking

Alex Appliance Repair services only microwaves integrated with a wall oven in a built-in combination unit. A microwave over a range, a countertop model, a microwave drawer or a standalone microwave in a trim kit is outside our service scope.

Microwave Runs but Does Not Heat

Record whether the controls, timer and turntable respond, and whether the separate oven compartment works. Several faults can produce a no-heat symptom. A technician needs to identify the model and inspect it before recommending a heating component or control repair.

Door, Display and Turntable Problems

Describe a latch that will not close, an unresponsive keypad, an error message or a turntable that does not move. Do not force the door or defeat an interlock. Consult the owner's manual for normal settings such as the control lock; do not open electrical panels.

When to Stop Using the Appliance

Stop use if there are sparks, smoke, an electrical smell or door damage. Do not run repeated tests. Internal microwave components require professional handling even after power has been disconnected.

Compare Repair with the Installed Replacement Cost

A built-in combination appliance is not equivalent to an inexpensive countertop microwave. Consider the condition of both compartments, cabinet dimensions, parts availability, removal and installation costs, and the technician's estimate. There is no universal age limit or repair-price percentage that makes the decision for every unit.

Prepare for Diagnosis

Provide the complete model number if accessible without moving the appliance, the symptoms and any error messages. Mention whether the microwave section, wall oven or both are affected and describe cabinet access.
