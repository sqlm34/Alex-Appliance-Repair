5 Built-in Microwave/Oven Combo Problems: Safe Next Steps

Five microwave-section problems in built-in microwave/oven combos: heating, starting, turntable, sparking and controls. Learn what to report before service.

Book service onlineMore repair guides

This guide concerns the microwave section of a built-in microwave/wall-oven combination appliance. Our service does not cover over-the-range, countertop, drawer or standalone built-in microwaves.

1. Microwave Runs Without Heating

Note whether the display and timer work and whether the oven compartment operates normally. A no-heat symptom can have more than one cause; do not order internal parts based on the symptom alone.

2. Microwave Will Not Start

Check the owner's manual for the normal control-lock setting and note any error message. Report whether the whole combination appliance is unresponsive or only the microwave section. Do not bypass a door switch or open the control panel.

3. Turntable Does Not Move

If your model uses a turntable, follow its manual to check the seating of removable tray parts with the appliance off and cool. If the problem remains, describe it when requesting diagnosis rather than accessing the drive components.

4. Sparking, Smoke or an Electrical Smell

Stop using the appliance and do not repeat a cycle to reproduce the fault. Report what you observed and any visible cavity or door damage. Internal inspection is a professional task.

5. Controls or Display Do Not Respond

Record the displayed code, which buttons are affected and whether the problem is intermittent. Mention any recent power interruption or repair. This information helps the technician plan model-specific diagnosis.

What Not to Do Yourself

Do not remove microwave covers, inspect internal wiring, replace high-voltage components or defeat interlocks. Do not pull the combination unit from its cabinet for a model photograph. Microwave components require professional handling even after disconnection.
