About the business

Practical service built around the actual failure

Alex Appliance Repair is the customer-facing trade name operated by Aksenov LLC, a local Indiana appliance repair business. We service household kitchen and laundry appliances at customer locations throughout our published service area.

The goal is straightforward: collect useful details before the visit, test the appliance instead of guessing, explain what was found and complete approved work with respect for the home.

The service call is $89 and is waived when the quoted repair is completed. Completed repairs include a 12-month parts and labor warranty under the applicable service terms.

What customers can expect

Standards that guide each appointment

Prepared scheduling

Brand, model, symptom and address information help us plan the route and research the appliance.

Measured diagnosis

Operating checks, visual inspection and applicable electrical, temperature, airflow or water tests support the recommendation.

Clear estimate

The failure and repair option are explained before approved work begins.

Home protection

Work areas are protected and appliance access is planned around the installation.

Final verification

The appliance is tested under applicable conditions after the repair.

Documented warranty

Completed repairs are backed by a 12-month parts and labor warranty under the service terms.
